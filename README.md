# AEG Construction Ltd

## Sustainable Building and Whole-House Retrofit Resources

AEG Construction Ltd is a UK-based construction and retrofit contractor supporting energy-efficiency improvements in existing buildings.

Our work includes whole-house retrofit planning, building insulation, low-carbon heating systems, solar photovoltaic installations and coordinated property upgrades.

## What We Focus On

- **Whole-house retrofit:** Understanding how building fabric, heating and ventilation interact.
- **Energy-efficient buildings:** Reducing avoidable heat loss through appropriate insulation and building improvements.
- **Low-carbon technology:** Considering heat pumps and renewable energy systems within a wider property improvement strategy.
- **Retrofit coordination:** Supporting structured assessment, planning and quality management.

## Open Educational Resources

We use this GitHub organisation to share practical digital tools, checklists and technical learning resources relating to building energy efficiency.

### UK Home Retrofit Planning Checklist

A browser-based educational resource to help users organise initial considerations before discussing property improvements with qualified professionals.

The checklist covers:

- Existing insulation and building condition
- Heating systems and controls
- Ventilation and moisture considerations
- Renewable energy opportunities
- Project priorities and next steps

This resource is for general education. It does not replace a professional retrofit assessment, technical design or regulatory advice.
